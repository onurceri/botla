# Installation Guide

## Placement

Mount each Beacon at a height of 1.1 to 1.7 metres, which matches the breathing zone of seated and standing people. Keep the sensor at least 2 metres away from windows, doors and ventilation outlets, and out of direct sunlight.

Use one sensor per room. In open-plan spaces, use one sensor per 50 square metres of floor area.

## Pairing

Install the Halvane mobile app, sign in, and scan the QR code on the back of the sensor. The sensor enters pairing mode automatically the first time it is powered on.

## Warm-up

Readings are available within a few minutes of pairing. CO2 readings reach full accuracy after the first automatic baseline calibration cycle has completed.
