# Halvane Instruments: Company Overview

Halvane Instruments ApS is a Danish company that builds indoor air-quality monitoring for schools, offices and public buildings. The company was founded in 2019 in Aarhus, Denmark, by Ingrid Solberg and Tomas Rehn. Ingrid Solberg serves as Chief Executive Officer and Tomas Rehn serves as Chief Technology Officer.

Halvane's product platform is called Halvane Beacon. It consists of wireless Beacon sensors that are mounted in rooms and the Halvane Console, a web dashboard where facility teams see live readings, set alert thresholds and export reports.

As of January 2025 Halvane had 84 employees. The headquarters is in Aarhus and the company operates a sales office in Rotterdam, the Netherlands. Halvane serves roughly 1,900 customer organizations in 14 countries, most of them in the Nordics, the Benelux region and Germany.

In 2022 Halvane raised a Series A round of 11 million euros led by Nordlys Ventures. The company states that its mission is "healthy air in every room people learn and work in."
