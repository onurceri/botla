# Pricing and Plans

Halvane Beacon subscriptions are priced per sensor per month and billed monthly by default.

## Starter

- 9 euros per sensor per month.
- Up to 25 sensors.
- Halvane Console dashboards, email alerts and Slack/Microsoft Teams alerts.
- Email support.
- No API access.

## Business

- 7 euros per sensor per month, with a minimum of 25 sensors billed.
- Everything in Starter, plus SAML single sign-on (SSO), REST API access, Zapier and Power BI integrations, and live chat support.

## Enterprise

- Custom pricing, minimum 250 sensors.
- Everything in Business, plus the on-premises Halvane Gateway, BACnet and Modbus building-management integrations, and a dedicated customer success manager.

## Discounts

- Annual prepayment: 15% discount on Starter and Business.
- Education: accredited schools and universities get a 20% discount on the Business plan.
- Discounts do not stack. When more than one discount could apply, the larger discount is used.

## Hardware and trial

Beacon S2 sensors are sold separately at 149 euros per unit. Hardware is included at no charge with a 3-year Business commitment. New customers can start a 30-day free trial with up to 3 loaner sensors.
