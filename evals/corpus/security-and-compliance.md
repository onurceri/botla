# Security and Compliance

## Certifications and audits

Halvane has been certified to ISO/IEC 27001 since 2023. A SOC 2 Type II report is available under NDA to Business and Enterprise customers. An independent external firm performs a penetration test of the platform every year.

## Identity and access

- Multi-factor authentication with TOTP authenticator apps is available to all users.
- SAML 2.0 single sign-on (SSO) is available on the Business and Enterprise plans.
- SCIM user provisioning is available on the Enterprise plan only.

## Responsible disclosure

Security researchers can report vulnerabilities to security@halvane.example. Halvane aims to acknowledge reports within 2 business days.
