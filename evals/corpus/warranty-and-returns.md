# Warranty and Returns

## Hardware warranty

Every Beacon sensor comes with a 24-month hardware warranty starting on the delivery date. The warranty covers manufacturing defects and sensor failures. It does not cover batteries, physical damage, water damage or devices that have been opened.

## Returns

You can return hardware within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Devices must be returned in their original packaging. Return shipping is paid by the customer, except for defective units.

## Requesting a replacement (RMA)

To request a warranty replacement, open the Halvane Console and go to Devices, select the affected sensor and choose "Request replacement". After the RMA is approved, the replacement is shipped within 3 business days. Shipping for warranty replacements is free within the EU, the United Kingdom and Norway.

Advance replacement, where the new unit is shipped before the faulty unit is returned, is available on the Enterprise plan only.
