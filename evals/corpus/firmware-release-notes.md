# Beacon Firmware Release Notes

Firmware updates are installed over the air automatically between 02:00 and 04:00 local time. Business and Enterprise customers can defer an update by up to 14 days.

## 4.4 (September 2025)

- Added per-room alert schedules, so alerts can be muted outside opening hours.

## 4.3 (June 2025)

- Fixed a battery drain bug that occurred when the Wi-Fi signal was weak. The bug was introduced in firmware 4.2. After updating, normal battery life is restored.

## 4.2 (March 2025)

- Added Thread 1.3 support.
- The sampling interval is now configurable from 1 to 15 minutes.

## 4.1 (November 2024)

- Improved fan scheduling for the particulate matter sensor to reduce noise.
