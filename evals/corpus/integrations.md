# Integrations

## Alerts

Slack and Microsoft Teams alert integrations are available on all plans. Alerts are sent when a reading crosses a threshold you configure in the Console.

## Automation and analytics

- Zapier: Business and Enterprise plans.
- Power BI connector: Business and Enterprise plans.

## Building management systems

BACnet/IP and Modbus TCP integrations require the on-premises Halvane Gateway and are available on the Enterprise plan only.

## Home Assistant

A Home Assistant integration exists but it is maintained by the community. It is not officially supported by Halvane, and Halvane support cannot help with it.
