# Support and Service Level Agreement

## Support channels

- Email (support@halvane.example): all plans.
- Live chat: Business and Enterprise.
- Phone: Enterprise only.

Standard support hours are Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 17:00 CET. Enterprise customers have 24/7 coverage for priority 1 (P1) incidents.

## Response targets

- Starter: 2 business days.
- Business: 8 business hours.
- Enterprise: 1 hour for P1 incidents and 4 hours for P2 incidents.

## Uptime SLA

The uptime SLA applies to the Halvane Console and the API and is measured monthly.

- Business: 99.9% monthly uptime.
- Enterprise: 99.95% monthly uptime.
- Starter: no uptime SLA.

If uptime falls below the SLA, the customer receives a service credit of 10% of the monthly fee for each full 0.1 percentage point below the SLA, capped at 30%. Credits must be claimed within 30 days of the end of the affected month.
