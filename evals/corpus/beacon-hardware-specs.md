# Beacon S2 Hardware Specifications

The Beacon S2 is the current Halvane sensor. It launched in September 2023 and replaced the earlier Beacon S1, which is no longer sold.

## What the Beacon S2 measures

- Carbon dioxide (CO2) with an NDIR sensor. Measurement range is 400 to 5,000 ppm with an accuracy of plus or minus (30 ppm + 3% of reading).
- Temperature and relative humidity.
- Particulate matter: PM2.5 and PM10.
- Total volatile organic compounds (TVOC).

The Beacon S2 does not measure radon, formaldehyde (HCHO) or noise levels.

## Power

The Beacon S2 runs on two AA lithium batteries or on USB-C power. Battery life is up to 18 months at the default sampling interval of 5 minutes. Shorter sampling intervals reduce battery life.

## Connectivity

The Beacon S2 connects over Wi-Fi on the 2.4 GHz band only; 5 GHz networks are not supported. Thread 1.3 support was added in firmware 4.2.

## Physical

The sensor has a 2.9 inch e-ink display, measures 92 x 92 x 31 mm and weighs 160 g. It is rated for indoor use only (IP20) with an operating temperature range of 0 to 40 °C.
