# Community Forum Digest

This page summarizes popular threads from the Halvane community forum. Posts are written by customers and are not official Halvane documentation.

## Placement tips from teachers

Several teachers reported that sensors mounted behind the teacher's desk gave more representative classroom readings than sensors next to the door.

## Battery tips

Forum members recommend lithium AA batteries. Members who used alkaline batteries reported roughly half the battery life.
